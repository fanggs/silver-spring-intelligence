ABOUT CIVICLENS

Silver Spring's public data, in plain English.

CivicLens is a tool built by UX University as an MVP for Silver Spring, Maryland. It brings open city and county data together in one place anyone can use: ask a question, get a short answer, and see the evidence behind it.

Ask a question

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, every time

  1. 1

    You ask

    Type a question in plain English, about Silver Spring or a neighborhood by name.

  2. 2

    We look it up

    CivicLens turns it into a database query and runs it, read-only, against public data.

  3. 3

    You see the evidence

    A short answer, a map and the tables behind it, with every source and the exact query.

Every number in an answer comes from public data, and code checks each one before you see it. If the data can't answer, CivicLens says so instead of guessing.

Ask whether a neighborhood fits a business, who lives and works nearby, how an area compares with the rest of Silver Spring, or whether permits, crime or crashes are rising.

THE DATA

Where the answers come from

  • U.S. Census Bureau

    Community survey estimates, jobs located in each area, business counts by industry

  • Montgomery County

    Permits, reported crime, crashes, parking, food inspections, rents, alcohol licenses, bus stops

  • Montgomery Planning

    Approved development projects and plan areas

  • OpenStreetMap

    Mapped businesses, venues and transit stations

Good to know: neighborhoods are drawn from whole census areas, so their edges are approximate. Map listings come from volunteers and can be incomplete. Census figures are survey estimates, and Census counts sex, not gender identity.
