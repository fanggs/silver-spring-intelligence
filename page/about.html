ABOUT

What is CivicLens?

CivicLens is a tool built by UX University (UXU) as an MVP for Silver Spring, Maryland. It brings open city and county data together in one easy-to-use place that anyone can use.

It is built mainly for small and medium businesses, entrepreneurs, people starting or serving businesses in the area, government workers, and anyone helping build their community.

How it answers

Ask a question in plain English. CivicLens writes a database query, runs it against public data, and writes the answer from what comes back. Every number in an answer comes from that data, and each answer lists its sources and the exact query it ran, so you can check it.

The data includes Census estimates, jobs and business counts, county records such as permits, crashes and reported crime, and mapped businesses from OpenStreetMap.
